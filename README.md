Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-office-jokes-dfd55a5a).

`main.py` (Python 3, pure stdlib, deterministic, no network) writes one
office joke — 124 characters — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> A coworker messaged 'quick question' at 9am — it's 4pm, we're on question eleven, and neither of us remembers the first one.

The joke turns on the "quick question" every office worker knows: one message
that spirals into eleven questions, and by the end nobody remembers what the
first one even was.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
